# Quotely Android downloads

**Download the current APK:** [Quotely Android preview 17](https://github.com/jeesry22-afk/Invoice-App-Downloads/raw/refs/heads/main/Quotely-Android-v1.0.0-preview.17.apk)

This school project preview includes taller At a glance cards and an Invoices Remaining count with its unit, readable full-width totals with At a glance icons on the left, document filters and status counts in one scrolling row with one selection at a time, Maya/GoTyme PH/GCash choices on the simulated payment page, and a reject action on quotation links. The payment page lets the client choose a simulated provider and records the fixed remaining invoice balance; no money is transferred. Invoices and quotations now send automatically from Quotely with a confirmation sheet and animated success feedback. The sender profile picture comes from the Quotely Google account and its appearance depends on the recipient email app. Settings has a bottom Log out button with a clear account confirmation, quotation creation has a searchable client list, and invoice send success offers a direct return to Home. The quotation review page has clearer approval and rejection actions.

On Android, open the APK after downloading it and allow installation from your browser or file manager if prompted. You can install this version over the previous preview without uninstalling. Keep a copy of important records because in-app backup and restore are not yet available.

Preview 17 includes each client's currency, saved demo conversion rates on quotations and invoices, a Demo card option with browser-only sample CVC and expiration, and email receipts after simulated payments. Preview 17 keeps Google sign-in and cloud records. Client currency now has the same field spacing as Phone, and page tips no longer appear during routine navigation; the first-use tour and Settings replay remain available. If Business setup opens for a saved session, it shows the signed-in email and lets you switch Google accounts without clearing app data. Android backup of app data is disabled and saved business profile drafts are removed after logout. This preview is signed with an Android development key, not a production Play Store release.
